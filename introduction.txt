##  Why I Joined Project Kappa
I am looking forward to bridging the gap between mathematics and technology. In math, I work with abstract logic, truth tables, and proofs on paper. Building a computer allows me to turn those concepts into physical objects.

Logic Gates: The Boolean algebra I study becomes physical hardware. When I wire up NAND or NOR gates, I am literally building physical truth tables.
The ALU: I get to help design the Arithmetic Logic Unit—the "brain" of the chip. This is where abstract binary code turns into actual addition and subtraction.

My Main Goal:I am looking for practical ways to apply diverse mathematical knowledge to solve problems in the current technological world.
